# Finken — BUILD PROMPT PACK

## GLOBAL BUILD PRINCIPLES

These instructions govern the entire project. Preserve them throughout all subsequent prompts.

### Product identity

Build **Finken**, a personal and small-business financial management application.

- Brand tagline: **Your Money, Made Simple.**
- Product positioning: **Know your money. Manage your money.**
- Core philosophy: **Simple on the surface. Intelligent underneath.**
- Product category: Personal and Small Business Financial Management.
- Currency default: PKR (Rs.), configurable for other currencies.
- Primary market context: Pakistan, but the product should be internationally adaptable.

Finken is NOT traditional accounting software. It is a friendly, intelligent financial companion.

Its product philosophy is:

**Capture → Organize → Understand → Plan → Act → Improve**

The application should help users answer:

1. Where is my money?
2. Where is it going?
3. What's coming up?
4. What can I afford?
5. What should I do next?

### Target users

Support two primary contexts:

**Personal**
- Individuals and families.
- Income and expenses.
- Household budgets.
- Savings goals.
- Loans and money owed.
- Bills and recurring payments.
- Shared expenses.

**Business**
- Freelancers, entrepreneurs and small businesses.
- Business income and expenses.
- Customers and suppliers.
- Receivables and payables.
- Business budgets.
- Cash flow and basic profit and loss.
- Business reports.

Both contexts must use the same underlying Money Engine.

### Spaces architecture

Use Spaces to separate financial environments.

Examples:
- Personal
- Family
- Business
- Car
- Side Business
- Company A

Every Space has its own transactions, categories, budgets, goals, people, loans, receivables, payables and financial insights.

Users can switch between Spaces without creating separate Finken accounts.

### Important product boundaries

Do not turn Finken into:
- A complex accounting system.
- An ERP.
- An inventory management system.
- A payroll system.
- A tax management system.
- An investment or cryptocurrency platform.

Avoid complex accounting terminology and unnecessary data entry.

### Technical principles

- Build a responsive web application, optimised for both mobile and desktop.
- Use React and TypeScript.
- Use reusable components and a modular folder structure.
- Use Tailwind CSS or an equivalent consistent styling system.
- Use a clean data-access layer separating UI components from business logic and storage.
- Use browser persistence for the prototype, preferably IndexedDB or an appropriate local persistence solution.
- Provide realistic seeded demo data.
- Make all primary interactions functional.
- Ensure data remains available after refreshing the browser.
- Avoid unnecessary external API dependencies.
- Do not require real banking, payment gateway or third-party integrations.
- Use mock authentication for the prototype, without pretending that it is production-secure.
- Keep the architecture ready for a future Firebase or other backend implementation.
- Never hardcode financial calculations separately in different screens. Use shared business logic.
- Do not replace existing working functionality when adding a new feature.

### Functional expectations

Every button, form, navigation item, filter, modal, dropdown and action must work.

Do not create decorative controls that appear functional but do nothing.

Use realistic empty states, loading states, confirmation messages, validation and error messages.

Financial calculations must be internally consistent.

If an action affects money, balances, budgets, receivables or payables, ensure all relevant views update accordingly.

### Design direction

Create a premium, modern FinTech + Productivity experience.

The interface should feel:
- Simple.
- Friendly.
- Trustworthy.
- Modern.
- Calm.
- Intelligent.

Use:
- Clean typography.
- Generous spacing.
- Rounded cards.
- Subtle borders and shadows.
- Restrained gradient accents.
- Clear financial numbers.
- Simple icons.
- Attractive but readable charts.
- Subtle transitions and micro-interactions.

Avoid:
- Traditional accounting software layouts.
- Excessive tables.
- Too many colours.
- Overcrowded dashboards.
- Unnecessary gradients.
- Excessive animations.
- Complicated navigation.

Do not make the logo a literal coin, wallet, calculator or rupee symbol. Use a clean typographic Finken wordmark initially. Do not invent a final logo identity.

**4. Money Owed**

- Borrowing.

**5. Commitments**

- Transaction date.
