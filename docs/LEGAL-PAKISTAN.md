# Legal questions for Pakistan

**This is not legal advice.** It is the list of questions to take to a lawyer qualified in Pakistan before launch, with what Finken does today so they can answer quickly. Laws and their status change; the lawyer should confirm what is in force on the launch date.

## What Finken is, for the lawyer

- A personal and small-business money tracker. People record their own income, spending, debts and goals.
- Finken does not hold, move or transfer money. It does not connect to banks, give credit, or give investment advice. Amounts are what people type in.
- Records are kept on servers (hosting provider and region not chosen yet), with a copy on the person's device. Sign-in is through a third-party provider (not chosen yet).
- People can record details of others: names and optional phone numbers of people who owe them money or whom they owe, customers and suppliers.
- People can download everything and delete their account in the app ([PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)).

## Questions

**Personal data**
1. Which personal data protection law applies at launch? Pakistan has had a draft Personal Data Protection Bill for several years; is it in force, and if not, what is the expected date and what should we follow until then?
2. Are financial records "sensitive" personal data under it, and what does that require (consent wording, localisation, registration, a data protection officer)?
3. Must data about Pakistani users be stored in Pakistan, or may it be hosted abroad? If abroad, under what conditions?
4. People record details of third parties (who owes them money). Do we need anything for that, such as terms saying users may only record what they are entitled to?
5. How quickly must we answer requests for a copy or deletion, and is our in-app download and deletion enough?
6. Is keeping backups 35 days after an account is deleted acceptable? Is keeping 30 days of request logs without personal details acceptable?
7. What must we do after a data breach: whom do we tell, and how fast?

**Cybercrime and records**
8. What does the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 (and its amendments) require of us: keeping traffic data, answering lawful requests, and how to verify them?
9. If an authority asks for a user's records, what process must they follow, and may we tell the user?

**Money and business**
10. Does any State Bank of Pakistan rule apply to a tracker that never handles money? At what point would one apply (for example the planned payment, invoicing or banking integrations in the roadmap)?
11. Business users record sales and costs. Does Finken have any duty under tax law (FBR) to keep or report records, or is that entirely the business's own?
12. If the export is used by an accountant or for tax, do we need any disclaimer about accuracy?

**Consumers and terms**
13. Which consumer protection laws apply (they are provincial), and what must the terms and privacy policy say? In Urdu as well as English?
14. Minimum age for an account?
15. For paid plans later: rules for subscriptions, renewals, refunds and taxes on digital services.

**Company**
16. Which company must operate the service and own the data, and must it be registered in Pakistan?
17. Any registration or approval needed before launch (for example with PTA for an app, or as a software exporter)?

## What we need back

- The privacy policy and terms of service, and the consent text for sign-up.
- Any change to what we keep, where, and for how long ([PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)).
- The process for lawful requests and for data breaches, with who is responsible.
- Whether hosting abroad is allowed, before the hosting provider is chosen.
